Dongho Kim

M.S. in Statistics & Data Science, Northwestern University
Applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027

My research starts from one question: how do we know whether an AI system actually works for the people who use it? I'm fortunate to be advised by Prof. Han Liu in the MAGICS Lab at Northwestern, where I co-developed SPINE. I also collaborate with the NSF–Simons SkAI Institute on time-series foundation models and agents for astronomy.

Research interests

Reliable AI & sociotechnical evaluation
Evaluating AI systems in the settings where people actually use them, not only on benchmarks.
Human–AI collaboration
How people and AI systems work and decide together, and when the combination does better than either alone.
Computational social science
Studying social systems with computational methods, and increasingly with AI itself.
